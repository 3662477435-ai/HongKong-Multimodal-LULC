# Data availability

The shareable study inputs, spatial splits, trained checkpoints, evaluation
source data and final shareable prediction products are publicly available
in [GitHub Release v1.0.0](https://github.com/3662477435-ai/HongKong-Multimodal-LULC/releases/tag/v1.0.0). The ten attachments include
split ZIP archives, release_manifest.json, SHA256SUMS.txt, a download helper
and README_DOWNLOAD.md. See GITHUB_RELEASE.md for the merge and extraction
workflow. All ten server-reported SHA-256 digests match the packaged files.

Google Earth RGB and mixed 3D-GloBFP/SinoBF-1 building features are withheld
pending component-specific redistribution permission. Their exact required
filenames, hashes and provider acquisition routes are in data_manifest.csv
and DATA_ACCESS.md. Complete frozen-model inference requires the same
rasters obtained lawfully; metrics from archived confusion matrices do not.
Third-party material retains its original terms (THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

No DOI is assigned. Figshare record 34937653 remains an unpublished draft,
not a public access route. Metadata inside the downloadable source archive
records its pre-publication packaging state; metadata/published_release.json
in this GitHub repository records the verified remote publication outcome.
